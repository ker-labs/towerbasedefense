Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game application Tower Base Defense ("Application", "Game") created and operated by Ker Labs.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in user privacy. The Game does not require you to create an account, register, or provide personally identifiable information (such as your real name, address, or phone number) to play.

A. On-Device Game Data

The Application stores gameplay progress locally on your device, including:

  • Level completion, high scores, and earned stars.
  • Tower upgrades, technology tree unlocks, and game settings (audio toggles, language preferences).

This data remains strictly stored on your device and is not transmitted to our personal servers.

B. Information Collected by Third-Party Services

To support the development and maintenance of the Game, we use third-party advertising services provided by Google:

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): We display optional rewarded video advertisements. AdMob may collect and process information such as:
    • Device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / AAID).
    • IP address and general coarse location.
    • Ad interaction and performance metrics.
    • App diagnostics and crash information.

3. Children's Privacy

Our Application does not knowingly address or collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information through our Game, please contact us immediately so we can take necessary actions.

4. Security

We value your trust in providing us your information. Any data collected by integrated third-party SDKs is transmitted using industry-standard encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any modifications are effective immediately once published on this page.
